Groove Trainer — macOS
======================
This folder is the Mac build kit. A .dmg cannot be compiled on Windows;
run this on a Mac with Python 3.11+, PyQt6, and Xcode command-line tools.

1. Copy the whole groove-trainer project onto the Mac.
2. chmod +x packaging/macos/build.sh
3. ./packaging/macos/build.sh

The sun icon (app_icon.icns) is used for:
- Groove Trainer.app in Finder and Dock
- the .dmg volume
- the installer window

Output: dist_installers/macos/GrooveTrainer-macOS.dmg
